How we use your email

Short version: we only email you about PhireD KTH 2027, and we delete your details after the event.

What we collect

Your email address and whether you're a PhD student, postdoc, master's student, company representative or other, when you fill in the sign-up form on this site.

What we use it for

  • Telling you the date and when the programme is published
  • A reminder shortly before the fair
  • One short feedback form after the fair

We don't send newsletters, sell your details or pass them to exhibitors.

Why we're allowed to

You give your consent by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us, and we'll remove you from the list.

Where it's stored and for how long

Sign-ups are stored in [Google Forms and Google Sheets], accessible only to the organising team. We delete the list by [31 May 2027].

Who is responsible

Dr/THS, the PhD Chapter at KTH (part of THS, the KTH student union), Stockholm. Contact: business@dr.kth.se.

You have the right to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. If you think we handle it wrongly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Fonts

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers when you visit.
