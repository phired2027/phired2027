A bridge between academia and industry

PhireD has always had one aim: to connect the people doing doctoral research at KTH with the organisations that need their skills. Run by the PhD Chapter, it brings industry, start-ups, research institutes and funders to campus for one afternoon.

  • For companies

    Find talent with doctoral degrees, and see the research directions coming out of KTH.

  • For PhDs and postdocs

    Find jobs and internships in industry, during your PhD or after you graduate.

  • For both

    Start conversations that can lead to collaborations, industrial PhD projects and joint research.

    Questions

    When and where?

    Tuesday 2 February 2027, 13:00–15:00, at Nymble, the student union house on KTH Campus, Drottning Kristinas väg 15 (map).

    Who can attend?

    PhD students and postdocs at any stage, from KTH and other universities.

    Does it cost anything?

    No. Entry is free, and so are the workshops.

    Do I need to register?

    Yes, please register with the KTH form. It helps us plan, and we'll send you a reminder the week before. Workshops have their own sign-up, linked in the calendar.

    Who will I meet?

    Companies, research institutes such as RISE and FOI, start-ups, and funders such as the Swedish Research Council and SSF. Many bring people from their R&D teams.

    How can my organisation exhibit or sponsor?

    See the packages for companies, or email business@dr.kth.se.

    Organised by PhDs, for PhDs

    PhireD KTH is run by volunteers from Dr/THS, the PhD Chapter at KTH. 2027 is the third edition.
