Questions

Who can attend?

PhD students and postdocs from KTH and other universities. Master's students thinking about a PhD are welcome too.

Does it cost anything?

No. Entry is free.

Who will I meet?

R&D staff from companies, research institutes such as RISE and FOI, and funding agencies such as the Swedish Research Council. Some bring recruiters too.

How can my organisation take part?

Email business@dr.kth.se for exhibitor and sponsor information.

Is it in English?

Yes, all talks and workshops are in English.

Organised by PhDs, for PhDs

PhireD KTH is run by volunteers from Dr/THS, the PhD Chapter at KTH, led by the chapter's Business Liaison. It started in 2025; in 2026 the team added funding agencies and pre-event workshops.
