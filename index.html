From thesis to job offer.

A one-day career fair where PhD students and postdocs talk to the people in companies' R&D teams, not only to recruiters.

Talk research with the people who do it

At most career fairs, companies send HR. That's fine for a first job, but it's hard to explain a thesis on hydrogen reduction kinetics to someone who can't tell you what the lab down the corridor works on.

At PhiRED, every company brings people from its R&D team: the scientists and engineers you'd work with. You can talk about methods, open problems and what research looks like in industry, and they can see what you bring beyond a CV.

Questions

Who will I meet from the companies?

People from their R&D teams: research scientists, engineers and team leads. Some companies also bring a recruiter. Each company's profile says who's coming.

Who can attend?

PhD students and postdocs from any university. Master's students who are considering a PhD are welcome too.

Does it cost anything?

No. Visiting PhiRED is free.

Do I need to register?

Not to visit the fair. Leave your email above to get the date, a reminder before the fair and the programme.

How can my company take part?

Email hello@phired.se and we'll send you the exhibitor information. You can also send us your company profile right away.

Will the event be in English?

Yes. All talks and the panel are held in English.
