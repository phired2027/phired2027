    Questions

    When and where?

    Tuesday 2 February 2027, 12:00–15:00, at Nymble, the student union house on KTH Campus, Drottning Kristinas väg 15 (map).

    Who can attend?

    PhD students and postdocs at any stage, from KTH and other universities.

    Does it cost anything?

    No. Entry is free, and so are the workshops.

    Do I need to register?

    Yes, please register, so we know how much fika to order. Workshops will have their own sign-up once they are announced.

    Who will I meet?

    Last year: companies such as ABB and Ericsson, research institutes such as RISE and FOI, start-ups, and funders such as the Swedish Research Council and SSF. Many brought people from their R&D teams. The 2027 exhibitors will be listed as they confirm.

    How can my organisation exhibit or sponsor?

    See the packages for companies, or email business@dr.kth.se.
