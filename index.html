A bridge between academia and industry

PhireD has always had one aim: to connect the people doing doctoral research at KTH with the organisations that need their skills. Industry, start-ups, research institutes and funders come to campus for one afternoon.

  • Academia side

    For PhDs and postdocs

    Explore jobs and internships in industry, during your PhD or after you graduate.

    • Talk research with scientists and engineers, not only recruiters
    • Meet funding agencies and research institutes
    • Free entry
    Browse exhibitors →

  • At PhireD

    Meet people face to face. A good conversation can be the start of an internship, a job or a research collaboration.

    • Free workshops in the weeks before the fair
    • Fika and networking on the day
    Get ready with our workshops →
  • Industry side

    For companies and funders

    Meet doctoral researchers in person, and see the research directions coming out of KTH.

    • Talk with PhD students and postdocs
    • Send us a short description of your research, and we'll share it on our exhibitors page
    • Free base package in 2027, with Silver and Gold options

    Questions

    When and where?

    Tuesday 2 February 2027, 13:00–15:00, at Nymble, the student union house on KTH Campus, Drottning Kristinas väg 15 (map).

    Who can attend?

    PhD students and postdocs at any stage, from KTH and other universities.

    Does it cost anything?

    No. Entry is free, and so are the workshops.

    Do I need to register?

    Yes, please register with the KTH form. It helps us plan, and we'll send you a reminder the week before. Workshops have their own sign-up, linked in the calendar.

    Who will I meet?

    Companies, research institutes such as RISE and FOI, start-ups, and funders such as the Swedish Research Council and SSF. Many bring people from their R&D teams.

    How can my organisation exhibit or sponsor?

    See the packages for companies, or email business@dr.kth.se.
